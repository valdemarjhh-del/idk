How weights and variants work

Simple option: Banana / 2 adds Banana with weight 2.

Group with variants: an unindented parent line sets the group's chance; indented lines are the possible results within that group.

Apple / 5
  Apple, Green / 2
  Apple, Yellow / 5
  Apple, Red / 3
Banana / 2

In this example, Apple has an overall weight of 5. Its variants are picked in a 2:5:3 ratio. A weight of 0 disables that option.
